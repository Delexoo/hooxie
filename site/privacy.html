Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026.

This page says what Hooxie handles, and what stays on your computer.

On your computer

The widget, your prompts, attached files, voice text, and screen shots are handled locally and then sent to the agent you configured, such as Cursor with your own API key. Hooxie's website does not receive the text of your prompts. Project files are not uploaded to the Hooxie site.

What we receive

  • The email address you type for billing, which Stripe stores as the customer email.
  • Whether this computer's one-time payment is complete, the payment amount, and the promotional code Stripe redeems. Card numbers go to Stripe, not to Hooxie.
  • Messages you email to support.

Payments

Stripe processes the one-time payment. Their privacy policy covers card data, receipts, and fraud checks. Hooxie uses that information to know whether this computer is licensed and to apply a promotional code.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your prompts to train a model. We do not put an advertising tracker on this site.

How long

Stripe keeps billing records for as long as their policy and the law require. A support email is kept long enough to answer it and handle a follow-up. You can ask us to delete a support thread.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@delexo.store.
